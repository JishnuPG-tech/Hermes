Client Setup Guides

Universal (Android, iOS, Windows, Mac):

  1. Install the Hiddify app from the Google Play Store or App Store.
  2. Copy the VLESS link above to your clipboard.
  3. Open Hiddify and click Add Profile or the + button.
  4. Select Import from Clipboard.
  5. Tap the big circle button to connect!

Android Client:

  1. Install v2rayNG from the Play Store.
  2. Copy the VLESS link above.
  3. Open the app, tap the + icon at the top, and select Import config from Clipboard.
  4. Tap the imported config to select it, then tap the V button at the bottom right to connect.

iOS/macOS Client:

  1. Open Shadowrocket on your iOS device.
  2. Copy the VLESS link or tap "Show QR Code" and scan it using the scanner icon at the top left.
  3. The app will automatically import the profile.
  4. Turn on the connection switch at the top.
